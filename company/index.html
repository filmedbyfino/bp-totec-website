Mission

The methodology is not new.
The market is underserved.

Bloomberg applied structured data to capital markets. BlackRock's Aladdin applied it to institutional risk. BlackPrint applies it to commercial real estate in Latin America.

The principle is the same: decisions improve when data is structured. The gap in LATAM CRE was not a lack of data — it was a lack of synthesis. We built BlackPrint to close that gap.

Principles

What we believe.

01

Decisions improve when data is structured.

The core principle. Not a feature. A belief that shapes everything from how we build the data layer to how we structure a demo session.

02

Built for the market, not adapted.

Every dataset, every feature, every workflow was designed with LATAM CRE in mind. Not retrofitted from a platform built for Chicago or London.

03

The analytical rigor of a Bloomberg terminal or Aladdin system, designed for the teams making real estate decisions in Mexico and Latin America.
