The Decision

Risk that isn't visible in a spreadsheet.

Investment decisions on commercial real estate assets in Latin America carry a structural challenge: the data required to assess location quality is fragmented, inconsistently available, and rarely structured in a format that investment committees can evaluate directly.

The result is due diligence that takes weeks, relies on 3rd-party reports with variable methodology, and produces assessments that are difficult to compare across a portfolio.

BlackPrint compresses that process without compressing the rigor.

What BlackPrint Shows You

All the variables that determine long-term asset performance — in one view.

Market Context

Demographic trajectory & income trends

Population growth, income segmentation evolution, and daytime population dynamics in the asset's trade area. Not a snapshot — a directional read on whether the market is strengthening or softening.

Demand Intelligence

Category demand trends

Which retail and commercial categories are underserved in the corridor. Where consumer demand signals are growing. Which categories face structural oversupply. Demand context that doesn't appear in cap rate analysis.

Competitive Dynamics

Competitive density & new supply pipeline

Current competitor count and format density within the trade area. New development permits and competitive supply coming to market. Exit signals — locations that have closed and what their departure reveals about the submarket.

Regulatory Risk

Zoning stability & development constraints

Zoning classifications and permitted use stability. Pending regulatory changes that affect asset use. Development pressure signals — construction permits, upzone discussions, and land use reclassification activity near the asset.

In Practice

12 assets. 4 days. Investment committee ready.

The situation

A real estate investment fund conducting due diligence on a 12-asset retail portfolio in Mexico. Traditional due diligence required 3rd-party reports taking 6–8 weeks per market. The timeline was not workable for the deal structure.

How BlackPrint structured it

The fund's analyst team loaded all 12 locations simultaneously. Demographic trajectory, competitive density change over 24 months, zoning stability, and vacancy context — structured into a single comparative view across the portfolio. Analysis ran in parallel, not sequentially.

The outcome

Due diligence compressed from 8 weeks to 4 days. Three assets flagged for elevated risk: demographic decline in two, accelerating competitor entry in one. One asset identified as undervalued — competitive exit signals in its trade area that weren't visible in the original deal deck. Investment committee received structured, asset-by-asset analysis with explicit risk categorization. Acquisition proceeded on 9 of 12 assets, with structured rationale for each exclusion.
