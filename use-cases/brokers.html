In Practice

8 sites. 2 off-market. Client retained for the next 15.

The situation

A boutique commercial brokerage advising a national pharmacy chain on 8 site acquisitions across CDMX. The client's previous advisory relationships had been transactional — brokers presenting listed inventory. The client wanted a firm that could identify opportunities the market hadn't yet priced.

How BlackPrint structured it

The firm used BlackPrint to scan corridors where competitor density had dropped and foot traffic had increased — signals invisible to standard MLS searches. Three off-market opportunities surfaced in corridors where a competing pharmacy chain had recently exited. Two were contacted directly before going to market. The firm presented all 8 sites with structured analysis: demographic alignment, foot traffic by hour, competitive density, and zoning confirmation.

The outcome

Two off-market sites were acquired at below-market terms. The client attributed the advisory quality directly to the data depth — something they had not previously received from a brokerage relationship. The firm retained the mandate for the client's next 15 locations. The advisory model has since been replicated across 4 other client relationships.
