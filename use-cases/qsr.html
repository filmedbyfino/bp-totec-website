The Problem

Expansion at scale amplifies every mistake

The faster you open, the more consequential each individual decision becomes. Errors in territory logic don't stay isolated — they compound across your network.

Risk 01

Cannibalization

New units opened too close to existing locations pull revenue from the same customer base. The growth metric looks positive; unit-level economics tell a different story.

Risk 02

Competitive saturation

Submarkets that appear underserved at the city level are often already saturated at the corridor level. Without format-specific competitive density data, you can't see it until you're already operating.

Risk 03

Inconsistent criteria

Expansion decisions made by different teams, in different cities, against different data sets produce inconsistent results. What works in one market gets misapplied in another.

The problem isn't that QSR and franchise operators lack discipline. It's that the data infrastructure to apply consistent criteria across an entire expansion pipeline hasn't existed — until now.
