The Problem

Expansion decisions don't fail from lack of experience.
They fail from lack of structure.

01 —

Site selection based on incomplete context

Most location decisions rely on visible signals: foot traffic, nearby stores, broker input. But the factors that determine whether a store outperforms or underperforms are rarely visible on the surface. Competitor saturation, mobility patterns, demographic alignment, zoning restrictions — these require structured data, not observation.

02 —

Analysis done once, not continuously

Markets shift. A location evaluated six months ago is not the same location today. Most teams conduct point-in-time analysis, then act on conclusions that have already aged. A structured intelligence system keeps analysis current as conditions change.

03 —

Data spread across disconnected sources

Demographics from one vendor. Traffic from another. Zoning from a government portal. Listings from a broker. Competitive density from a spreadsheet someone built manually. No single person, no matter how experienced, can synthesize this accurately at speed.

BlackPrint doesn't replace market experience. It gives that experience a structural foundation.

Why It Persists

The data exists. The problem is fragmentation.

Latin American CRE teams are not operating without data. They are operating with too many data sources, none of which speak to each other. Demographics live in one system. Mobility in another. Zoning in a government portal that was last updated two years ago. Competitive intelligence in a spreadsheet someone built manually.

The result is not a data problem. It is a synthesis problem. Teams cannot evaluate, compare, and validate decisions at the speed and consistency that modern expansion requires — not because they lack expertise, but because no single system gives them a unified view.

BlackPrint is built to solve exactly this. Not by adding more data. By structuring it.

How BlackPrint Works

150+ datasets. One analytical system.
From question to decision.

01 — Define

Choose your geography.

Choose a city. Select a territory. Search any address. BlackPrint gives you a precise geographic canvas — from a single parcel to an entire metropolitan corridor. Set capture areas by radius, walk time, or drive time. Stack multiple locations side by side.

02 — Analyze

Layer real data across your area.

Mobility and traffic patterns. Demographic density and income segmentation. Zoning classifications and cadastral boundaries. Competitive presence: brands, categories, ratings, opening dates. On-market and off-market commercial listings. 150+ structured datasets — in seconds, not days.

03 — Decide

Score, rank, and act.

Score and rank your opportunities. Compare locations objectively. Build a shared portfolio your team can evaluate together. Export analysis-ready reports for internal validation or executive review. Move from scattered data to aligned strategy — without changing your process, just structuring it.

Data → Decision → Strategy

What Changes

The cost of a failed store goes beyond rent. Relocation, brand damage, capital written off, and time lost. BlackPrint surfaces the structural risk factors — cannibalization, demographic misalignment, competitive saturation — before a decision is made, not after.

02

Decisions you can defend internally

In most organizations, a real estate recommendation is only as strong as the evidence behind it. BlackPrint produces structured, exportable analysis that allows expansion managers to bring data into executive conversations — not just instinct.
