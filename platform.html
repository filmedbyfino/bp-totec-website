Common Questions

Before you book.

What geographic markets does BlackPrint cover?

BlackPrint is built for Mexico and Latin America. Current primary coverage is across major Mexican metros — CDMX, Monterrey, Guadalajara, Querétaro, and expanding. Additional LATAM coverage is active and growing. Your walkthrough will confirm specific coverage for the markets you're evaluating.

How current is the data?

Dataset freshness varies by category. Mobility and traffic data refreshes continuously. Competitive intelligence updates weekly. Listings are synced daily. Demographic and zoning data follows government cadence with manual enrichment. Full freshness specifications are available on request during a demo session.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams are active on BlackPrint within one week. The platform is designed to be adopted without a data science team. Your walkthrough session includes hands-on use in your actual markets — you leave the session knowing how to use it, not just what it does.

Can I export analysis for internal presentations?

Yes. BlackPrint supports structured PDF export and data export formats suitable for executive presentations and board-level reviews. Reports are formatted to show the analytical rationale behind a recommendation — not just a summary, but the evidence chain.

What is the pricing model?

Pricing is discussed during your walkthrough session, where we can confirm the right coverage tier for your geography and team size. We do not publish fixed pricing because the right structure depends on your markets and use case. No commitment is required from the demo session.
