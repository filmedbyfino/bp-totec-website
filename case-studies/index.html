Retail Expansion · CDMX

5 candidate sites. 4 days. 2 sites approved with structured rationale.

The Challenge

A regional specialty food retailer needed to evaluate 5 candidate sites across Mexico City before Q1 board sign-off. Their process relied on broker recommendations and manual research. Timeline: 6 weeks budgeted. Quality: inconsistent.

How BlackPrint Structured It

BlackPrint loaded all 5 sites simultaneously. Demographic alignment, traffic patterns, competitor density, and on-market comparables — visible in a single view. Two sites were eliminated in day one based on demographic misalignment. The remaining three were ranked by composite score.

Outcome

Decision made in 4 days. Two sites approved. One held for Q2 pending a competitor exit signal flagged by the platform. The board presentation included structured, exportable analysis — not a broker deck.

QSR Network Expansion · Monterrey

30-location network. 4-site entry sequence. Zero cannibalization impact.

The Challenge

A QSR franchise operator with 30 existing locations needed to evaluate Monterrey for network expansion. The question was not whether to enter — it was where, in what sequence, and how to avoid cannibalizing their existing Nuevo León presence.

How BlackPrint Structured It

City-wide market segmentation across Monterrey's 9 primary corridors. Competitor format density by submarket. Drive-time cannibalization modeling against existing locations. The data produced a recommended 4-location entry sequence, prioritized by market gap and saturation risk.

Outcome

3 of the 4 recommended locations opened in Q1. The cannibalization model proved accurate — zero material revenue impact on existing network. Internal team now uses BlackPrint for all expansion sequencing decisions.

Investment Due Diligence · Guadalajara

6-asset portfolio. 8 weeks compressed to 6 days. 4 acquisitions with structured rationale.

The Challenge

An investment fund conducting due diligence on a 6-asset retail portfolio in Guadalajara. Traditional due diligence timeline was 6–8 weeks per cluster. The fund needed to move faster without compromising the depth of market analysis.

How BlackPrint Structured It

All 6 assets analyzed simultaneously. Demographic trajectory, competitive density change over 24 months, zoning stability, and vacancy context — structured into a single comparative view. Two assets flagged for elevated risk (demographic decline, increasing competitor entry). One asset identified as undervalued based on competitive exit signals in its trade area.

Outcome

Due diligence compressed from 8 weeks to 6 days. Investment committee received structured, asset-by-asset analysis with explicit risk categorization. Acquisition proceeded on 4 of 6 assets, with structured rationale for each exclusion.

The pattern across every case: decisions made faster, with more structure, and with less dependence on information that was difficult to validate. The data existed in each case. BlackPrint structured it before the decision was made.
