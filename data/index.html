Methodology

How the data is built.

Aggregated from primary sources

BlackPrint aggregates data from municipal governments, mobility platforms, commercial property databases, and proprietary collection methods — not from secondary data resellers or international platforms built for other markets.

Structured and validated continuously

Data is continuously reconciled for accuracy and freshness. Competitive data refreshes weekly. Listings data refreshes daily. Demographic and cadastral data is reconciled against primary sources on a rolling basis.

Built for LATAM decision contexts

Every dataset is structured around the questions that commercial real estate teams in Latin America are actually asking — not adapted from a US or European platform where different variables drive different decisions.

BlackPrint aggregates, structures, and validates data from municipal governments, mobility platforms, commercial property databases, and proprietary collection methods. Data is continuously reconciled for accuracy and freshness.
